Roadmap

The work

  • Mirror2

    A Rust-powered photobooth built for what happens after the smile. Layer custom high-powered filters, shoot the glass, the street, the face in the mirror — and the reflection stops behaving. Edges drift. Light turns wrong. The more you push, the more paranormal it gets. A camera app where the interesting part is on purpose.

  • New Tower Shell

    Mohawk’s younger command-line brother. A power CLI for RSS and the open web — subscribe, crawl, grep, and pipe feeds through your shell like data, not decoration. Follow threads across sites, fork what you find, and explore the news graph without waiting on a window. For readers who want the raw wire, faster than the app.
